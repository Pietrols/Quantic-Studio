## Scope, ranking and price conventions

Collected from the power engine notes and benchmark gaps, mine-rules inventory,
PV dossiers/resource/engine requirements, circuit dossiers, automation dossier and
power exchange research at main 567003e. A named source may be already owned but
unread, public but unobtained, paid, or edition-unresolved. Those are distinct.
Every entry below names the blocking evidence, issuer/edition where established,
what it unblocks and the free/owned route. U means unresolved, never zero cost or
proof that no public price exists. Unidentified source families stay unidentified.

Ranking is our inference from mining-study priorities in
[power requirements](power/reconstruction-requirements.md):

- Rank 0: establish the applicable Zambian legal/connection baseline using public
  instruments and existing copies first. These are access/verification tasks.
- Rank 1: fault/protection/arc, cable and earthing evidence for first mining work.
  After release, select the smallest actual project-dependent set, not all rows.
- Rank 2: drive harmonics, motor fitting, islanded dynamics and reliability.
- Rank 3: broader planning, PV/circuit/automation depth and exchange completeness.

All numeric prices are public observations on **2026-10-18**, in original currency,
not quotes, total landed costs or exchange-rate estimates. IEC base publications,
amendments, CSV, RLV and CMV are different products. Do not add an amendment twice
when included in a consolidated version. Checkout format, users, tax, region and
rights require rechecking after release. Indexed prices are labelled indexed.
Prices U were not reliably retrieved, even where a purchase route exists.
