## 1. Low-voltage minimum factor

Expected from the version-tagged documentation: c_min=0.95 below 1 kV for both
6% and 10% tolerance. Actual v3.5.6 `_add_c_to_ppc`: 0.95 for 6%, 0.90 for 10%.
This is a factor difference, not solver convergence error. Holding Thevenin
impedance fixed, 0.90 gives 5.2631579% less current than 0.95.

  (`_add_c_to_ppc`)

## 2. Thermal current frequency

Expected from the documented far-from-generator equation:
`Ith = Ikss * sqrt(1+m)`, where
`m = expm1(4*f*T*ln(kappa-1)) / (2*f*T*ln(kappa-1))`.
Actual v3.5.6 `_calc_ith` fixes f=50 even when net.f_hz=60.

For the authored one-bus source below, R/X=0.1 and T=0.1 s:
- 50 Hz: engine 33.4202129132 kA; equation 33.4202129132 kA.
- 60 Hz: engine 33.4202129132 kA; equation 32.7125959255 kA.

## 3. Minimum-case line resistance

The branch-elements documentation prints 0.04/K, while installed
`build_branch._end_temperature_correction_factor` explicitly uses 0.004/K.
The mismatch was already recorded in CC-1 ADR-0003 and its source entry.
At a synthetic final temperature of 80 C, the two formulas give resistance
multipliers 3.4 and 1.24 respectively. These are not interchangeable.

Source: https://pandapower.readthedocs.io/en/stable/shortcircuit/branch_elements.html
(Line), and installed pandapower 3.5.6 `build_branch.py`, function named above.
